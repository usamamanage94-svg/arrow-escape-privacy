Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Last updated: October 2026

Arrow Escape ("the app") is a puzzle game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what happens with information when you use the app.

We do not collect, store or share any personal information ourselves. Your game progress, best stars and settings are saved only on your device and never sent to us.

See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

The app is not directed to children under 13.
